# S Device — Future Phone Project

Budget-friendly rebuild of an existing damaged Samsung smartphone into a functional, futuristic Android device.

## Project phases
1. Hardware inspection, battery safety, exact model confirmation and backup.
2. Professional screen/back-glass repair; bench functional checks.
3. S Device Android launcher and AI experience (no system flashing required).
4. Optional external low-voltage microdisplay and optical 3D illusion.
5. Prototype enclosure, testing and evidence records.

## Engineering boundaries
- A photograph does not establish exact phone model or working condition.
- Optical 3D illusions and screen-based AR are not free-space volumetric holograms.
- Keep original motherboard and safe battery/charging system.
- Never attach bare wires to lithium batteries; do not puncture, heat or dismantle them.
- No custom ROM or bootloader unlock before backup and model-specific compatibility are verified.
- Do not upload IMEI, serial numbers, personal photos, or credentials to public evidence.
