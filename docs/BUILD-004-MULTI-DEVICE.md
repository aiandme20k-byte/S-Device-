## Build policy
1. Each phone has its own model-specific hardware BOM, inspection report, bill of repair, and independent functional check.
2. Do not cross-wire different phone motherboards, Li-ion batteries, flexible screen cables, or charging circuits.
3. Reversible S Device Android launcher APK is first software target; test on intact Realme before Samsung/Infinix installations.
4. A cracked external glass is not automatically a damaged display. Decide glass-only versus full assembly based on image, touch, lamination, exact model and repair risk. OCA lamination/separation is specialized equipment work; do not use household heat guns or liquid adhesives.
5. Identify swelling and sharp exposed glass before handling or charging; disconnect power and seek trained repair if swollen, punctured or overheating.
6. Photos of powered-on screens do not certify all camera, sensor, battery or network functions.
7. AI and visual effects are features achievable with apps; free-space holography and transparent replacement screens are speculative goals until bench-demonstrated.

REGISTER -> EXTERNAL SAFETY -> BACKUP -> HARDWARE DIAGNOSIS -> REPAIR -> SOFTWARE INSTALL -> FEATURE TEST -> ENCLOSURE -> ACCEPTANCE.
